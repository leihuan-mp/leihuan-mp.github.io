I am an Associate Professor in the [Department of Computational Mathematics, Science, and Engineering](https://cmse.msu.edu/) at Michigan State University, with a joint appointment in the [Department of Statistics and Probability](https://stt.natsci.msu.edu/). I received my Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics from Brown University under supervision of Prof. George Karniadakis.

My research lies at the intersection of computational mathematics, multiscale modeling, and scientific machine learning. I develop computational methods for constructing partial differential equations (PDEs) and stochastic models of multiscale or high-dimensional problems from microscopic first-principles descriptions. In particular, my work focuses on the characterization of admissible model classes that preserve conservation laws, dissipative structure, and physical constraints as the reduced description is enriched. 
I construct representations that enable fast numerical algorithms and develop learning methods for identifying their parametric form from microscopic laws and data. The central goal is to construct physically interpretable reduced models that retain microscopic model fidelity and admit provably stable, structure-preserving, and efficient  numerical approximation. My recent work includes multiscale fluid dynamics, kinetic transport and coarse-grained molecular systems, particularly in regimes where conventional closures shows limitations.
